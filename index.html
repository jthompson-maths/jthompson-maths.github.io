Hello

I am currently a postdoctoral researcher in the Department of Mathematics and Statistics at the University of Western Australia.

My research interests are in nonlinear and/or nonlocal partial differential equations arising from phase transitions and shape optimisation. In particular, I am interested in free boundary problems, nonlocal minimal surfaces, and overdetermined problems.

In July 2025, I was awarded my PhD from the University of Western Australia under the supervision of Enrico Valdinoci (principal supervisor), Serena Dipierro, and Giorgio Poggesi.
